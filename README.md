# 🍲 Pet Puja Community 

**Bridging the gap between students and hygienic local mess services.**

[![Live Demo](https://img.shields.io/badge/demo-live-orange)](https://petpuja.vercel.app)
[![GitHub](https://img.shields.io/badge/repo-github-blue)](https://github.com/AachleshMule/pet-puja-app)

## 🚀 The Mission
Pet Puja was born from a simple observation: millions of students and young professionals live away from home and struggle daily to find hygienic, affordable, and "home-style" meals. While big delivery apps focus on high-end restaurants, the local "Mess" ecosystem remains digitally invisible. 

**Pet Puja Community** is a mobile-first digital bridge that empowers local vendors and provides students with transparency, trust, and ease of access to healthy daily meals.

---

## 🧩 The Problems We Solve

### 1. The Trust & Hygiene Gap
* **Problem:** Students rely on word-of-mouth to find messes, with no way to verify hygiene standards or daily menus beforehand.
* **Solution:** A professional digital storefront that brings local vendors into the digital-first era, offering clear visibility into their offerings.

### 2. Decision Fatigue
* **Problem:** Traditional food apps are cluttered with thousands of options, making it hard to find a simple "Daily Thali" quickly.
* **Solution:** A streamlined UI focused on "Today's Menu," allowing users to decide and order in under 60 seconds.

### 3. Digitizing Small Vendors
* **Problem:** Small, high-quality mess owners lack the technical expertise or margins to join major delivery platforms.
* **Solution:** A community-centric platform with low barriers to entry, designed specifically for the "Mess" business model.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
* **Frontend:** React.js (Vite)
* **Routing:** React Router DOM v6
* **Styling:** Custom CSS3 (Mobile-First Responsive Design)
* **Icons:** React Icons (FontAwesome, Material Design)
* **Deployment:** Vercel

---

## 📱 Features
- **Seamless Navigation:** Fully implemented routing from Welcome -> Sign In -> Home -> Restaurant Menu -> Cart.
- **Dynamic Menu Rendering:** Real-time-ready layouts for daily specials like Mess Thalis and snacks.
- **Mobile-First Experience:** Optimized for use on smartphones, mirroring a native app feel.
- **Order Flow:** Interactive cart summary and checkout transition.
